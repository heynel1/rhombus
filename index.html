Key learning points — open after writing

A rhombus is a quadrilateral with four equal sides. It also has two pairs of parallel opposite sides and equal opposite angles. Turning it does not change these properties. A square is a special rhombus. Parallel sides or equal opposite angles alone are not enough to identify a rhombus.

Teacher notes and SLS setup
Designed as a 15-minute consolidation followed by a 5-minute exit ticket. Explore is optional support within the consolidation. All diagrams are schematic; use their given measurements and markings.
Upload the ZIP as a Text/Media HTML5 resource. This package gives local feedback and scores; it does not send marks to the SLS gradebook. For a teacher-visible record, add an SLS Free-Response question and ask pupils to paste the learning record there. Review reflection against the three criteria: accurate property, specific evidence, and explanation of improved reasoning (0–3, one mark each). The pupil checklist is self-assessment, not an automatic teacher mark.
Questions 1–4 are automatically marked /4. Questions 5–6 are teacher-reviewed /3 each: correct judgement, relevant property, and evidence linked to the judgement. Reflection is teacher-reviewed /3. Use submitted records to identify reliance on appearance, square exclusion, adjacent/opposite-angle confusion, and insufficient properties. There is no live teacher dashboard; collect records through a native SLS response.
